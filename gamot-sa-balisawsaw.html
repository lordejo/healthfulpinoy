Medical Overview

Normal ba ito o may mas malalim na dahilan? Ang balisawsaw ay may medical term na “dyusiria” o pag ihi ng pa maya maya ngunit kokonti lamang.

Madalas na mapag kakamalan itong UTI o Urinary Trunk Infection.

May mga scientific na paliwanag ito na mag papliwanag sa atin ng dahilan ng balisawsaw.

Ang balisawsaw ay maaring tawaging simple o ordinary lamang at hindi nangagailangan ng gamutan.

May balisawsaw din naman na dahilan ng pag kakaroon ng mas malalang sakit.

Sa pamamagitan ng regular na check-up o urinalysis ay matitiyak natin ang dahilan ng balisawsaw.

Urinary incontinence is an underdiagnosed and underreported problem that increases with age, affecting 38-55% of women older than 60 years and 50-84% of the elderly in long-term care facilities. At any age, urinary incontinence is more than twice as common in females than in males.
